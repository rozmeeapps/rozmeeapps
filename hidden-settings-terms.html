02 / WHAT THE APP DOES

A shortcut to screens your phone already has

The App lists Android settings screens, checks which of them exist on your phone, and opens the one you tap. Those screens are part of Android or your phone maker's software, not part of the App.

The App never changes a setting by itself. Any change you make on a screen it opens is made by you, in your phone's own settings, and you are responsible for it.

03 / ADVANCED SETTINGS

Screens marked “Be careful” or “Advanced”

Some screens, such as developer options, access point names (APN), reset options, trusted certificates, installing unknown apps and phone testing menus, can affect how your phone, network or apps work. The App marks them and shows a warning before opening advanced screens.

  • Only change what you understand. If you're not sure what an option does, leave it as it is.
  • Reset options can erase data. Resetting network settings, app preferences or the whole phone can remove saved Wi-Fi networks, settings, apps or personal data. Back up your phone before any reset.
  • Network and testing menus can change how your phone connects to mobile networks. Wrong values can stop calls or mobile data from working until you change them back.
  • Security screens such as unknown app installs, device admin apps and trusted certificates protect your phone. Only allow apps and certificates you trust.
04 / DIALER CODES

Information-only codes

The App lists dialer codes that are meant to show information, such as your IMEI or a testing menu. We deliberately leave out codes known to reset, wipe or reconfigure phones. Even so, codes behave differently between phone makers, models and mobile networks, and we can't guarantee what a code does on your phone.

The App never dials a code for you. “Open dialer” only types the code into your phone app, and the code runs only if you press call. Don't enter codes from other sources unless you trust them and understand what they do.

05 / COMPATIBILITY

Every phone is different

Which screens exist, what they're called and what they show depends on your phone maker, model, Android version and updates. A screen may be missing, may open a different page than described, or may stop working after an update. The App checks availability on your phone and marks screens it can't open as “Not on this phone”, but this check isn't perfect. Brand-specific screens, such as autostart or battery managers, are provided by phone makers and can change at any time.
