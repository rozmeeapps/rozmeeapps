01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hidden Settings app for Android and to this page. Both are provided by Rozmee (“we”, “us”, “our”). Hidden Settings lists useful Android settings screens, including ones that are hard to find, checks which of them exist on your phone, and opens them when you tap. It also offers favorites, a home screen widget, a Quick Settings tile, step-by-step guides, a list of information-only dialer codes and a developer options helper.

We designed Hidden Settings so that we never receive your data. You don't need an account. The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request Android's internet permission.

02 / WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect nothing

Rozmee does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data from the Hidden Settings app. Everything the app needs is processed and stored on your phone, and we cannot see it.

The only time we receive information about you is when you choose to email us (see Other apps & services and Contact).

04 / INFORMATION ABOUT YOUR PHONE

What the app reads, and why

  • Phone details. The app reads your phone's maker, model, Android version, security update date, memory and storage size, and whether developer options appear to be on. It uses them to show your phone on the home screen, to pick brand-specific screens and tips, and to count the settings that work. They are shown on screen only. They are not saved and never sent anywhere.
  • Which settings screens exist. To hide shortcuts that can't open on your phone, the app asks Android whether each screen in its list exists. For this, it can see only a fixed set of system and phone-maker apps that provide those screens, such as Android Settings, the Phone app, Digital Wellbeing and the device-care apps of Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo/Realme/OnePlus, Vivo, Huawei/Honor and Asus. The app does not request access to the list of all apps installed on your phone. The results are kept in memory only.
05 / PERMISSIONS

No permissions at all

Hidden Settings does not request any Android permissions. That includes internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS, storage, accessibility and “query all packages”. The home screen widget, app shortcuts and Quick Settings tile need no permissions either.

06 / OTHER APPS & SERVICES

When another app takes over

  • Settings screens. When you tap a setting, the app asks Android to open that screen. From then on you are in Android's Settings app or your phone maker's app, and their privacy policies apply. Hidden Settings never changes a setting by itself.
  • Dialer codes. “Open dialer” opens your phone app with the code already typed. Nothing is dialed until you press call yourself, and Hidden Settings can't see what the code shows. “Copy” puts the code on your clipboard.
  • Send feedback. This opens your email app with our address filled in, plus your phone model, Android version and app version to help us answer. You can edit or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you press send.
  • Email support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and help you, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.
  • Google Play Store. When you download the app, or tap “Rate” or “Share” inside it, the Play Store or your chosen sharing app takes over. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links you open. The open-source licenses screen can open the license text in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or external fonts.
07 / STORAGE, BACKUP & DELETION

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • Remove a favorite by swiping it away on the Favorites tab, or by tapping its star.
  • Clear your history from Settings → Data → Clear recently opened.
  • Clear your searches from Settings → Data → Clear search history.
  • Remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Backup: so your favorites survive a new phone, Hidden Settings lets Android include your favorites, recently opened list and preferences in your own Android backup and in device-to-device transfers. If you use Google backup, that copy is stored in your Google account under Google's terms. Rozmee has no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

08 / SECURITY

Keeping it safe

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server copy to be breached. Your favorites can appear on your home screen widget and in the app icon's shortcut menu, so anyone who can use your unlocked phone can see them. Keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

09 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because Hidden Settings data stays on your device, you can use these rights directly in the app by viewing or deleting your favorites, history and searches. We hold no app data about you that we could hand over.

For anything you've sent us by email, contact us and we'll respond within the time the law requires. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

10 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

Hidden Settings is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11 / CHANGES

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Hidden Settings adds features that change how data is handled, such as optional paid features, ads or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
