01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Hidden Settings app for Android and to this page. Both are provided by Rozmee (“we”, “us”, “our”). Hidden Settings lists useful Android settings screens, including ones that are hard to find, checks which of them exist on your phone, and opens them when you tap. It also offers search, favorites, a home screen widget, a Quick Settings tile, step-by-step guides, a list of information-only dialer codes and a developer options helper.

You don't need an account to use the app. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

02 / WHAT WE COLLECT

What information is involved

  • On your phone only: your favorites, recently opened settings, recent searches and preferences (see Data on your device).
  • Technical and usage information: if the version of the app you use includes Google Firebase services, limited technical information, app usage events and crash reports are sent to Google so we can improve the app (see Google Firebase).
  • Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include (see Contact).

We do not collect your name, contacts, messages, call logs, photos, files or precise location, and we do not record what you change inside the settings screens the app opens.

04 / INFORMATION ABOUT YOUR PHONE

What the app reads, and why

  • Phone details. The app reads your phone's maker, model, Android version, security update date, memory and storage size, and whether developer options appear to be on. It uses them to show your phone on the home screen, to pick brand-specific screens and tips, and to count the settings that work. Basic device information such as model and Android version may also be included in Firebase reports (see Google Firebase).
  • Which settings screens exist. To hide shortcuts that can't open on your phone, the app asks Android whether each screen in its list exists. For this, it can see only a fixed set of system and phone-maker apps that provide those screens, such as Android Settings, the Phone app, Digital Wellbeing and the device-care apps of Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo/Realme/OnePlus, Vivo, Huawei/Honor and Asus. The app does not request access to the list of all apps installed on your phone.
05 / GOOGLE FIREBASE

Usage, crash reports & notifications

Hidden Settings may use Google Firebase services to improve reliability, understand which features are used and deliver notifications. When a service below is included in the version of the app you use, it works as described:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. Collects app interactions (for example, screens viewed and features used), session information, app-instance identifiers, app version, device and Android version, language and approximate region. We use these reports to understand how the app is used and to improve it. We do not send your favorites list, search words or the contents of settings screens as analytics events. See Google's Analytics data-collection information.
  • Firebase Crashlytics. Sends crash traces, installation identifiers, device and app details and diagnostic context to Google so we can find and fix problems.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging. Uses installation identifiers and registration tokens to deliver app notifications. On Android 13 and later, notifications are shown only if you allow them.
  • Firebase Remote Config. Lets us adjust app settings and content (for example, turning a feature on or off) without an app update. It uses installation identifiers and basic device information.

Firebase sends information to Google rather than keeping it only on your device, and processing may take place outside your country. Google processes this data for us under its terms. See Firebase's privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy for details on processing, safeguards and retention.

06 / PERMISSIONS

Only what the app needs

Hidden Settings requests only the Android permissions it needs to work:

  • Internet and network state, so Firebase services can send usage information and crash reports and receive notifications and configuration.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later), only if the app sends notifications. You can turn them off at any time in Android settings.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS, call log, storage, accessibility or “query all packages” permissions. The home screen widget, app shortcuts and Quick Settings tile need no extra permissions.

07 / OTHER APPS & SERVICES

When another app takes over

  • Settings screens. When you tap a setting, the app asks Android to open that screen. From then on you are in Android's Settings app or your phone maker's app, and their privacy policies apply. Hidden Settings never changes a setting by itself.
  • Dialer codes. “Open dialer” opens your phone app with the code already typed. Nothing is dialed until you press call yourself, and Hidden Settings can't see what the code shows. “Copy” puts the code on your clipboard.
  • Send feedback. This opens your email app with our address filled in, plus your phone model, Android version and app version to help us answer. You can edit or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you press send.
  • Email support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and help you, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.
  • Google Play Store. When you download the app, or tap “Rate” or “Share” inside it, the Play Store or your chosen sharing app takes over. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links you open. Links to this policy, the Terms & Conditions or open-source license texts open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or external fonts.
08 / STORAGE, BACKUP & DELETION

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data on your phone stays there until you delete it. You can:

  • Remove a favorite by swiping it away on the Favorites tab, or by tapping its star.
  • Clear your history from Settings → Data → Clear recently opened.
  • Clear your searches from Settings → Data → Clear search history.
  • Remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Firebase data is kept by Google for the retention periods described in Google's documentation (for example, Analytics event data is kept for a limited period set in our Firebase project). Uninstalling the app stops any further collection.

Backup: so your favorites survive a new phone, Hidden Settings lets Android include your favorites, recently opened list and preferences in your own Android backup and in device-to-device transfers. If you use Google backup, that copy is stored in your Google account under Google's terms. Rozmee has no access to it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

09 / SECURITY

Keeping it safe

Data on your phone is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Information sent to Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections and protected by Google's security measures. Your favorites can appear on your home screen widget and in the app icon's shortcut menu, so anyone who can use your unlocked phone can see them. Keep your phone protected with a screen lock.

10 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can view and delete your favorites, history and searches directly in the app.

For information processed through Firebase or sent to us by email, contact us and we'll respond within the time the law requires. Firebase data is linked to app-instance identifiers rather than your name, so we may ask for details that help us find it. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

11 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

Hidden Settings is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.
