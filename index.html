Set up in seconds

You don't need a launcher or an account. Phone Widgets works with your phone's own home screen.

  1. Pick a widgetBrowse by category or open a theme set.
  2. Customise itChoose colours, content and size, with a live preview.
  3. Add to home screenTap Add and place the widget wherever you like.

Your data, your choices

Widget content is stored on your device. All permissions are optional, and you only need to allow the ones your widgets use. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app uses and why.
