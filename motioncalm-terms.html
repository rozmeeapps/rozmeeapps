02 / SAFETY FIRST

For passengers only

Never use MotionCalm while driving, cycling, or operating a vehicle or machinery. The App is designed only for passengers.

  • Cue overlay. Motion cue dots are drawn on top of other apps and can partly cover screen content. Adjust their size, opacity and position, or turn them off whenever they get in the way.
  • Stay aware. Pay attention to your surroundings, the crew and safety announcements, especially on planes, boats and public transport. Don't let the App distract you from safety instructions.
  • Sound. Keep the pre-trip tone, music and breathing sounds at a comfortable, low volume. Stop if anything feels uncomfortable.
  • Breathing and acupressure. Breathe gently and stop if you feel dizzy or short of breath. Apply only light pressure for acupressure, and don't press on injured, swollen or sore skin.
  • If you feel worse, stop using your phone, look at the horizon, and stop the activity. Get help from a professional if needed.
