Motion sickness relief for passengers

Use your phone on the move, without feeling sick.

MotionCalm puts small dots at the edges of your screen that move with the vehicle, so your eyes see the motion your inner ear already feels. Add guided breathing, calm music and a trip log that shows what helps you.

Get it on Google Play ↗ See how it helps
⚠

For passengers only. Never use MotionCalm while driving. MotionCalm is not a medical device. If symptoms are severe or frequent, talk to a doctor.

Six calm tools in one app

Use one tool or combine them. Each one is based on published research, and the app links to the sources so you can read them yourself.

⁘

Motion cues

Dots float over any app and follow acceleration, braking and turns from your phone's motion sensors. You can change the style, size, colour and sensitivity, and set them for a car, bus, train, plane or boat.

◌

Guided breathing

Slow, paced breathing with a visual guide, soft sound cues and gentle haptics, for when you start to feel uneasy.

∿

Pre-trip tone

A short, soft low-frequency hum to listen to before you set off, at a volume that feels comfortable.

♪

Calm music

Pleasant ambient music generated on your phone. It plays offline and can run together with the cues.

✚

Relief now

One tap opens quick steps for when you already feel sick, plus a P6 acupressure guide and practical travel tips.

▤

Private trip log

Rate how each ride went and see which tools work best for you. The log stays on your phone and you can export or delete it.

Starts when you need it

Turn cues on from the app, a Quick Settings tile or a home screen widget. You can also let them start on their own.

  1. Vehicle detectionThis is optional. When Android detects that you're riding in a vehicle, MotionCalm can start cues or send you a reminder.
  2. Car BluetoothThis is optional. Pick your car's Bluetooth, and MotionCalm will remind you to start cues when your phone connects.
  3. Look-up remindersGentle nudges to rest your eyes on the horizon every few minutes.

Nothing leaves your phone

MotionCalm has no account, no ads and no analytics, and the app does not request internet access. Sensor readings are used live and never saved. Your trip log is stored only on your device.
