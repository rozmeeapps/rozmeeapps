01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the RideMargin: Vehicle Motion Cues app for Android (also called MotionCalm, package com.ridemargin.motioncues.sickness) and to this website. Both are provided by Rozmee (“we”, “us”, “our”). MotionCalm is a travel-comfort app for passengers. It offers on-screen vehicle motion cues, guided breathing, a pre-trip tone, calm music, travel tips and a personal trip log.

You can use the app without an account. Versions with Firebase include automatic usage analytics, crash reporting and push registration. The core passenger tools work offline. Older versions without Firebase do not send app analytics, crash reports or push registrations.

02 / WHAT WE COLLECT

What connected features send

  • Usage analytics. Google Analytics for Firebase receives app interactions such as session starts and engagement time, an app-instance identifier, app version, device model, operating system and language. Google can derive an approximate location, such as city or country, from the connection’s IP address. We use this information to understand app usage and improve the app.
  • Crash reporting. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and app-not-responding reports, stack traces, timestamps, installation and session identifiers, app and OS versions, and device state such as available memory. This helps us reproduce errors and prioritise fixes. Automatically recorded events can give context leading up to a crash.
  • Push notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a Firebase installation identifier and a registration token to deliver messages to this copy of the app. Firebase also processes technical details about the app and device to operate delivery. Analytics may also measure notification interactions.

Google receives the network address needed to connect to its services. We use these services to operate messaging, understand usage and fix faults. The app shows no ads. Advertising ID collection and ad personalisation signals are disabled in the app. We do not sell personal information.

04 / PERMISSIONS

What each permission is for

Android grants internet access at installation. Features such as vehicle detection and cue overlays use additional permissions that you can manage separately.

  • Display over other apps. This draws the motion cue dots on top of whatever app you're using. MotionCalm only draws dots. It does not read, capture or record anything on your screen.
  • Notifications. These show the ongoing “cues are on” notification, look-up reminders, ride reminders and post-trip rating prompts. Android notification settings control whether push messages appear.
  • Physical activity (optional). This lets the app use Android's activity recognition to detect when you're riding in a vehicle, so cues can start or you get a reminder. Only the moment you enter or leave a vehicle is used, and it isn't stored except as the start of a trip in your log.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth (optional). This lists your paired Bluetooth devices so you can choose your car, and detects when your phone connects to it. MotionCalm does not scan for, connect to or track other devices.
  • Internet. The app connects to Google for the Firebase services described above.
  • Foreground services. These keep the cues and music running reliably while you use other apps.
  • Run at startup. This turns auto-start back on after your phone restarts or the app updates, if you enabled it.
  • Vibration. This is used for gentle haptics in breathing exercises and optional reminder vibrations.

MotionCalm does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or storage permissions.

05 / THIRD PARTIES

Services outside the app

  • Google Firebase and Google Analytics. Google processes the data listed above to provide analytics, crash reporting and message delivery. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google Analytics data safeguards.
  • Google Play services. Vehicle detection uses the Activity Recognition API in Google Play services, which runs on your device and is provided by Google. Google's handling of data in Play services is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The feature is off unless you turn it on.
  • Google Play Store. When you download the app, or tap “Rate” or “Share” inside it, the Play Store or your chosen sharing app takes over. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links you open. Links in the App, such as this policy and the Terms, open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • Exporting your trips. If you export your trip log, Android's file picker saves a JSON file wherever you choose, which might be a cloud drive. Once exported, that file is under your control and is subject to that service's terms.
  • Email support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and help you, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. Please don't send personal details we don't need.
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or external fonts.
06 / STORAGE & DELETION

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your local trip history stays on your phone until you delete it. Available controls include the following.

  • Delete a single trip from its detail screen.
  • Delete all trips from Settings → Your data → Clear trips.
  • Export your trips as a JSON file from Settings → Your data → Export trips.
  • Remove locally stored trips and settings by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

You can disable visible notifications in Android settings. Firebase registration, usage analytics and crash reporting remain active. Clearing trips or uninstalling removes local records only. Previously transmitted records follow each provider’s retention process. Analytics event-level retention depends on the property settings. Crashlytics starts removing crash traces and associated identifiers after 90 days. Firebase retains installation IDs used for messaging until deletion is requested and can take up to 180 days to remove them from live and backup systems. See Firebase retention details and Analytics data retention.

Backup: MotionCalm excludes its data from Google cloud backup. Android may still copy app data during a direct device-to-device transfer that you start yourself when setting up a new phone.

07 / SECURITY

Keeping it safe

Android protects the app’s local storage. Firebase and Analytics encrypt transmitted data in transit. Use a screen lock to protect trip notes from anyone who can access your phone.

08 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data and to restrict or object to processing. You can view, export and delete trip records in the app.

For questions or requests about support email or data sent to our service providers, contact rozmee.dev@gmail.com. We will explain what records we can identify and respond within the time required by law. Please do not include trip notes or other personal details in your request.

09 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

MotionCalm is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact Rozmee so we can investigate and address the request.
