01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the MotionCalm app for Android and to this website. Both are provided by Rozmee (“we”, “us”, “our”). MotionCalm helps passengers reduce motion sickness with on-screen motion cues, guided breathing, a pre-trip tone, calm music, tips and a personal trip log.

We designed MotionCalm so that we never receive your data. You don't need an account. The app has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request Android's internet permission.

02 / WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect nothing

Rozmee does not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data from the MotionCalm app. Everything the app needs is processed and stored on your phone, and we cannot see it.

The only time we receive information about you is when you choose to email us (see Third parties and Contact).

04 / PERMISSIONS

What each permission is for

MotionCalm asks for a permission only when you turn on a feature that needs it, and explains why first. If you decline, the rest of the app still works.

  • Display over other apps. This draws the motion cue dots on top of whatever app you're using. MotionCalm only draws dots. It does not read, capture or record anything on your screen.
  • Notifications. These show the ongoing “cues are on” notification, look-up reminders, ride reminders and post-trip rating prompts.
  • Physical activity (optional). This lets the app use Android's activity recognition to detect when you're riding in a vehicle, so cues can start or you get a reminder. Only the moment you enter or leave a vehicle is used, and it isn't stored except as the start of a trip in your log.
  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth (optional). This lists your paired Bluetooth devices so you can choose your car, and detects when your phone connects to it. MotionCalm does not scan for, connect to or track other devices.
  • Foreground services. These keep the cues and music running reliably while you use other apps.
  • Run at startup. This turns auto-start back on after your phone restarts or the app updates, if you enabled it.
  • Vibration. This is used for gentle haptics in breathing exercises and optional reminder vibrations.

MotionCalm does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, storage or internet permissions.

05 / THIRD PARTIES

Services outside the app

  • Google Play services. Vehicle detection uses the Activity Recognition API in Google Play services, which runs on your device and is provided by Google. Google's handling of data in Play services is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The feature is off unless you turn it on.
  • Google Play Store. When you download the app, or tap “Rate” or “Share” inside it, the Play Store or your chosen sharing app takes over. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Links you open. The “Science & sources” screen opens research papers in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.
  • Exporting your trips. If you export your trip log, Android's file picker saves a JSON file wherever you choose, which might be a cloud drive. Once exported, that file is under your control and is subject to that service's terms.
  • Email support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply and help you, and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose. Please don't send sensitive health details we don't need.
  • This website. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to serve and protect the site, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages use no cookies, analytics, ads or external fonts.
06 / STORAGE & DELETION

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete a single trip from its detail screen.
  • Delete all trips from Settings → Your data → Clear trips.
  • Export your trips as a JSON file from Settings → Your data → Export trips.
  • Remove everything, including settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Backup: MotionCalm excludes its data from Google cloud backup. Android may still copy app data during a direct device-to-device transfer that you start yourself when setting up a new phone.

07 / SECURITY

Keeping it safe

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Because nothing is transmitted, there is no server copy to be breached. Keep your phone protected with a screen lock, since anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app.

08 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because MotionCalm data stays on your device, you can use these rights directly in the app by viewing, exporting or deleting your trips. We hold no app data about you that we could hand over.

For anything you've sent us by email, contact us and we'll respond within the time the law requires. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

09 / CHILDREN

Children's privacy

MotionCalm is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10 / CHANGES

Changes to this policy

If a future version of MotionCalm adds features that change how data is handled, such as optional paid features, ads or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
